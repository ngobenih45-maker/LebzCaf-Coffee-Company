The name

Lebohang plus Café

The name LebzCafé is a frankenword of the words Lebohang or Lebza, and Café. Lebohang Nyandeni is our founder, and his name sits on every pouch.

Unlike overly serious coffee brands, we love fun. We tell South African stories because we relate to them, unlike anybody else.

Why it is bitter

South Africans are carrying a lot

South Africans are facing a lot of challenges. It can be harsh. It can be bitter.

  • Rising interest rates
  • Rising costs
  • Loadshedding
  • Umjolo
  • Waking up every morning to put food on the table

Your coffee is the one thing that should not add to it. That is why we made it 100% freeze dried, smooth and rich, in a cup that is ready in seconds.
